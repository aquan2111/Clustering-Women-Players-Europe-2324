#### **Breakdown of results by features**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

* Cluster 0 center backs' defensive actions happen lower, while they passed more on the ground and their passing lengths under pressure were less affected.
* On the other hand, cluster 1 center backs engage more highly, both in terms of receiving and defending, while attempting more forward and progressive passes into the final third.

Therefore, based on these results,

* Cluster 0 center backs can be seen as traditional defenders who tend to hold the defensive line.
* Cluster 1 center backs can be seen as stoppers who are more aggresive in stepping up for defensive actions.

<a id="results-for-3-clusters-breakdown-of-results-by-features"></a>

#### **Breakdown of results by features**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

* Cluster 0 shows more positiveness towards average positioning for receiving and defensive actions, and progressive forward passing.
* Cluster 2, on the other hand, shows more positiveness towards average pass length change under pressure, while engage in defensive actions at much lower positions.
* While for cluster 1, most of their features stayed in between the other two clusters, not much really stood out.

Hence, the following conclusions can be made:

* Similar to k = 2 cluster 0, k = 3 cluster 2 center backs can be seen as traditional defenders holding the line.
* Similar to k = 2 cluster 1, k = 3 cluster 0 center backs can be seen as stoppers.
* Cluster 1, while they engage in defending as highly as cluster 0, their passing look close to cluster 2, which can be seen as high engagement low progressing center backs.

However, these results are not stable as number of components are added, so results are not highly confident.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players"></a>

### **Breakdown of results by players**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

<a id="molly-bartrip-tottenham-hotspur"></a>

#### **Molly Bartrip - Tottenham Hotspur**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Bartrip was safely assigned to the holding the line center backs for both k = 2 and k = 3. She stood out for higher than average ground pass percentage, while most of her defensive actions happened very low down the pitch at low variance. Forward and into the final third passing were under average for her among her cluster.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-elba-verges-prats-sd-eibar"></a>

#### **Elba Verges Prats - SD Eibar**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Verges was safely clustered into stoppers for both k = 2 and k = 3. Her involvements both in defending and receiving happened higher up the pitch, while such locations showed more variance. Her passes were mostly low, forward, and into the final third rather than on the ground.

<a id="kathy-hendrich-vfl-wolfsburg"></a>

#### **Kathy Hendrich - VfL Wolfsburg**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Hendrich was closely clustered into line holding center backs for k = 2, and was also closely clustered into the high engagement low progressing center backs. On the one hand, most of her defensive actions took place lower down the pitch with less variance, except for tackles, interceptions, and clearances. On the other hand, she had a higher share of passes forward but not into the final third, while more of her final third entries were done via carrying.

<a id="sophie-howard-leicester-city"></a>

#### **Sophie Howard - Leicester City**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Howard was closely clustered into line holding defenders for both k = 2 and k = 3, and close also means she was close to other clusters as well. Her pressing and tackle positions were high, but her receipt and other defensive actions were low, even though her receipt positions saw somewhat more variance. Her passing was mostly forward, but her carries into the final third were less present.

<a id="risa-shimizu-west-ham-united"></a>

#### **Risa Shimizu - West Ham United**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Shimizu was clustered into the progressive stoppers for both k = 2 and k = 3, although margins were very close against other clusters. Although mostly known as a fullback, she spent significant time as a center back this season for West Ham United. Her passing saw high share forward and progressive, while her carrying saw high share into the final third, but her pressures applied right after losing the ball were low, her receiving and defensive actions were mostly at lower locations, apart from tackles, interceptions and recoveries.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-alex-greenwood-manchester-city"></a>

#### **Alex Greenwood - Manchester City**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Greenwood was clustered into progressive stopper cluster for k = 2, but was changed to high engagement low progressing center backs for k = 3. Her passing and carrying forward and into the final third stood out, her receipt and press and positions were high up the pitch, especially for her receipt positions which varied by more than average, while her tackle and block positions were lower, and she did not carry at high speed on average.

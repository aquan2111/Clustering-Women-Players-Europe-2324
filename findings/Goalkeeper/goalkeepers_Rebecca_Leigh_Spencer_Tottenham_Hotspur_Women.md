## **Findings**

Despite Tottenham being a mid table team, Spencer was quite safely clustered into ball playing keepers cluster. What made her stand out are her passing lengths on average shorter than average for keepers in the same cluster while her high ground pass percentage stayed in line with other players in this cluster, although her receiving and sweeping positions were lower than average for players of her cluster.

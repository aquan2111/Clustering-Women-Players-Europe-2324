## **Findings**

Earps was clustered into ball playing keepers' cluster for both k = 2 and k = 3. However, her assignments showed quite parity among all clusters in both scenarios, as her k = 2 margin reflects a genuine, if modest, lean toward Ball-Playing, while her k = 3 margin is where the true near-three-way-tie lives. Most of her passing styles, including passing lengths and percentage of passes on the ground, were similar to ball playing keepers' cluster, although the trends were not as clear as Spencer. She also stood out for much more advanced sweeping positions, which make her look close to Sweeper Keeper cluster.

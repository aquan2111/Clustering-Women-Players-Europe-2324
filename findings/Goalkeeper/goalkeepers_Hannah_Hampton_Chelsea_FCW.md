## **Findings**

Similar to her teammate at Chelsea, Hampton was also clustered into traditional keepers quite safely, although the margin was slightly less than Musovic. Similar to Musovic, Hampton also attempted longer passes, although lengths of passes under pressure do not differ by much than overall average. In addition, her share of passes on the ground was quite similar to Musovic, and when it is adjusted according to Chelsea's dominance, it also becomes low. However, what made Hampton different to Musovic is that she tended to receipt in more advanced positions, which made her look more modern than traditional.

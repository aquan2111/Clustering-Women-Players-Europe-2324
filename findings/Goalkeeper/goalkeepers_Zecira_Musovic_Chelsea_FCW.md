## **Findings**

For both k = 2 and k = 3, Musovic safely got clustered into traditional keepers. What stand out for her is longer pass lengths, especially under pressure. While attempting a higher than average share of passes on the ground than an average traditional keeper, when this metric is adjusted according to Chelsea's dominance, it becomes rather low.

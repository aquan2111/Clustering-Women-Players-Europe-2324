## **Findings**

Korenciova was clustered into k = 2 Ball Playing cluster and k = 3 Sweeper cluster, in spite of Como finishing only 7th out of 10 in Serie A Women that season. This can be explained by her sweeping and positioning pointing towards the average of players in sweeper cluster, even though the figures were still lower by quite a margin, while not relying not as much on ground passes.

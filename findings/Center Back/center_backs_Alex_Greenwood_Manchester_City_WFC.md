## **Findings**

Greenwood was clustered into progressive stopper cluster for k = 2, but was changed to high engagement low progressing center backs for k = 3. Her passing and carrying forward and into the final third stood out, her receipt and press and positions were high up the pitch, especially for her receipt positions which varied by more than average, while her tackle and block positions were lower, and she did not carry at high speed on average.

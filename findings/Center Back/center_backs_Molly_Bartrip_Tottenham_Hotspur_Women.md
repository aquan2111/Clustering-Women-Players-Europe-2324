## **Findings**

Bartrip was safely assigned to the holding the line center backs for both k = 2 and k = 3. She stood out for higher than average ground pass percentage, while most of her defensive actions happened very low down the pitch at low variance. Forward and into the final third passing were under average for her among her cluster.

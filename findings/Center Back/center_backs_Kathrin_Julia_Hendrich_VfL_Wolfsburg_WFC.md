## **Findings**

Hendrich was closely clustered into line holding center backs for k = 2, and was also closely clustered into the high engagement low progressing center backs. On the one hand, most of her defensive actions took place lower down the pitch with less variance, except for tackles, interceptions, and clearances. On the other hand, she had a higher share of passes forward but not into the final third, while more of her final third entries were done via carrying.

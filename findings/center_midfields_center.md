#### **Breakdown of results by features**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

* Cluster 0 midfielders made more progressive passes and passes into opposition box, carried more into the final third, defended at more advanced locations, in particular blocking and pressing.
* On the other hand, cluster 1 midfielders shot closer to goal at higher quality per shot, while standing out for percentage of header passes and shots. They received more inside opposition box, and their receiving locations varied more.

Therefore, based on these results,

* Cluster 0 center midfielders can be seen as progressive distributor.
* Cluster 1 center midfielders can be seen as aerial and box arrival threats.

<a id="results-for-3-clusters-breakdown-of-results-by-features"></a>

#### **Breakdown of results by features**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

* Cluster 1 midfielders passed and carried more backward, while stood out for header features like shooting and clearing. Their receipt positions varied more, while receipts inside the box also stood out. Their shots were closer to goal and of higher quality.
* While cluster 0 midfielders passed and carried more forward and progressive into the final third, where their passes were longer on average. Most of their positionings were higher up the pitch, including for receiving, dribbling, and most defensive attributes.
* Cluster 2 midfielders, on the other hand, involved in lower positions both when attacking and defending, while dribbling and receiving more centrally.

Hence, the following conclusions can be made:

* Similar to k = 2 cluster 1, k = 3 cluster 1 comprises of aerial/box arrival threats midfielders, which can be seen as deep aerial specialists. Whether these players can be defined as box to box midfielders need to depend on heat map of locations.
* Cluster 0 midfielders can be seen as progressive distributors and carriers, which can be seen as advanced playmakers.
* Cluster 2 can be seen as deep lying playmakers.

### **Breakdown of results by players**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-manuela-giugliano-as-roma"></a>

#### **Manuela Giugliano - AS Roma**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Giugliano was clustered confidently with progressive distributors at k = 2 and advanced playmakers at k = 3. Her forward progressive passes and carries stood out for players of this cluster, and the passes can include those into the final third or even into opposition box. The lengths of these passes are also longer. She was also involved at more advanced locations up the pitch.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-sjoeke-nusken-chelsea"></a>

#### **Sjoeke Nusken - Chelsea**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Nusken was safely clustered into cluster with aerial/box arrival threats for both k = 2 and k = 3. She stood out for receipts in opposition box, header shots and clearances, quality of shots at closer distance, and backward pass share. Her receipt and defending locations were further up the pitch.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-melanie-leupolz-chelsea"></a>

#### **Melanie Leupolz - Chelsea**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Leupolz was safely clustered into k = 2 progressive distributors cluster and k = 3 deep lying playmakers cluster. Her passes were mostly long, forward, progressive, and into the final third, but not into opposition box. She involved in lower locations down the pitch, and did not receive much inside opposition box and engage much in counterpressing in opposition half.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-missy-bo-kearns-liverpool"></a>

#### **Missy Bo Kearns - Liverpool**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Kearns was closely clustered into k = 2 progressive passers cluster, but then for k = 3 she was clustered with aerial/box arrival threats. She had moderate times of receiving in opposition box and header shots. Varying trends can be seen in her positioning, as she received, blocked, won fouls, and got dispossessed at higher locations, lower locations are places where she dribbled and made recoveries.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-lena-oberdorf-vfl-wolfsburg"></a>

#### **Lena Oberdorf - VfL Wolfsburg**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Oberdorf was closely clustered with progressive distributors at k = 2, but was then clustered with deep lying playmakers slightly more confidently at k = 3. Most of her involvement happened lower down the pitch, while she stood out for long progressive forward passes, which can be either final third entries or even into opposition box. In contrast, she also had significant header shots.

<a id="results-for-3-clusters-breakdown-of-results-by-features"></a>

#### **Breakdown of results by features**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

* Cluster 0 goalkeepers stand out for high share of passes on the ground, passes from keepers' arms, and lower sweeping positions. These keepers can be read as ball playing keepers.

* While cluster 1 goalkeepers, in contrast, sweep at higher positions, while passing share stays in the middle of the other two clusters. These keepers can be interpreted as sweeper keepers.

* Cluster 2 goalkeepers are characterized by higher average pass lengths including under pressure. Similar to k = 2 cluster 1, these keepers are more traditional than the other two types.

Once again, goalkeeper positioning and saving by body parts do not contribute to clustering, although sweeping positioning does.

However, these results are not robust as number of components are added, so results are not highly confident.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players"></a>

### **Breakdown of results by players**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-zecira-musovic-chelsea"></a>

#### **Zecira Musovic - Chelsea**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

For both k = 2 and k = 3, Musovic safely got clustered into traditional keepers. What stand out for her is longer pass lengths, especially under pressure. While attempting a higher than average share of passes on the ground than an average traditional keeper, when this metric is adjusted according to Chelsea's dominance, it becomes rather low.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-hannah-hampton-chelsea"></a>

#### **Hannah Hampton - Chelsea**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Similar to her teammate at Chelsea, Hampton was also clustered into traditional keepers quite safely, although the margin was slightly less than Musovic. Similar to Musovic, Hampton also attempted longer passes, although lengths of passes under pressure do not differ by much than overall average. In addition, her share of passes on the ground was quite similar to Musovic, and when it is adjusted according to Chelsea's dominance, it also becomes low. However, what made Hampton different to Musovic is that she tended to receipt in more advanced positions, which made her look more modern than traditional.

<a id="rebecca-leigh-spencer-tottenham-hotspur"></a>

#### **Rebecca Leigh Spencer - Tottenham Hotspur**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Despite Tottenham being a mid table team, Spencer was quite safely clustered into ball playing keepers cluster. What made her stand out are her passing lengths on average shorter than average for keepers in the same cluster while her high ground pass percentage stayed in line with other players in this cluster, although her receiving and sweeping positions were lower than average for players of her cluster.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-maria-korenciova-como"></a>

#### **Maria Korenciova - Como**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Korenciova was clustered into k = 2 Ball Playing cluster and k = 3 Sweeper cluster, in spite of Como finishing only 7th out of 10 in Serie A Women that season. This can be explained by her sweeping and positioning pointing towards the average of players in sweeper cluster, even though the figures were still lower by quite a margin, while not relying not as much on ground passes.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-mary-earps-manchester-united"></a>

#### **Mary Earps - Manchester United**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Earps was clustered into ball playing keepers' cluster for both k = 2 and k = 3. However, her assignments showed quite parity among all clusters in both scenarios, as her k = 2 margin reflects a genuine, if modest, lean toward Ball-Playing, while her k = 3 margin is where the true near-three-way-tie lives. Most of her passing styles, including passing lengths and percentage of passes on the ground, were similar to ball playing keepers' cluster, although the trends were not as clear as Spencer. She also stood out for much more advanced sweeping positions, which make her look close to Sweeper Keeper cluster.

## **Findings**

Ingle was confidently clustered into k = 2 cluster 0 comprising of deep defensive midfielders. Her passes were mostly forward and progressive, while her carries were not. One interesting observation is that she involved in receiving, dribbling, and pressing at higher locations than most deep defensive midfielders

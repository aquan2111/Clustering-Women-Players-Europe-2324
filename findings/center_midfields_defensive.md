#### **Breakdown of results by features**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

* Cluster 0 midfielders made more passes and carries forward, especially passes into the final third, won more fouls while defending and progress mainly with passes.
* On the other hand, cluster 1 midfielders made more passes backward on the ground, carried more into the final at higher speed, received and performed defensive actions at higher positions at greater variance, specifically with counterpresses in opposition half. They progressed more by carrying than cluster 0.

Therefore, based on these results,

* Cluster 0 center midfielders can be seen as deep defensive midfielders, which align closely to a traditional number 6 player in football.
* Cluster 1 center midfielders can be seen as more advanced dynamic defensive midfielders which can resemble a Segundo Volante.

<a id="results-for-3-clusters-breakdown-of-results-by-features"></a>

#### **Breakdown of results by features**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

* Cluster 2 midfielders made defensive actions at lower positions.
* Cluster 0 midfielders, on the other hand passed more forward and progressive into the final third, where their passes were longer on average, and passing was their main progression method, while carrying was forward but not progressive as they carried the least distance on average. Their defensive actions were taken at relatively higher positions than cluster 2.
* While for cluster 1, these midfielders attempted more passes backward and on the ground, but they received and defended at higher positions up the pitch.

Hence, the following conclusions can be made:

* Similar to k = 2 cluster 1, k = 3 cluster 1 comprises of more advanced dynamic defensive midfielders which can resemble a Segundo Volante.
* Cluster 0 defensive midfielders can be seen as deep progressive passers
* Cluster 2 can be seen as anchor holding defensive midfielders.

However, as number of components are varied, quite significant changes can be observed; therefore, these results presented are not highly confident.

### **Breakdown of results by players**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-sophie-ingle-chelsea"></a>

#### **Sophie Ingle - Chelsea**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Ingle was confidently clustered into k = 2 cluster 0 comprising of deep defensive midfielders. Her passes were mostly forward and progressive, while her carries were not. One interesting observation is that she involved in receiving, dribbling, and pressing at higher locations than most deep defensive midfielders

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-kim-little-arsenal"></a>

#### **Kim Little - Arsenal**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Little was confidently clustered into k = 2 cluster 1 comprising of more advanced dynamic defensive midfielders. She progressed more by carrying, and she carried longer distance at higher speed. Her attacking and defending involvement was mostly at higher locations up the pitch. She was also active with counterpressing.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-clare-wheeler-everton"></a>

#### **Clare Wheeler - Everton**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Wheeler was clustered with deep defensive midfielders, but the margin was thin. Her involvement locations were mostly low in both defending and attacking. However, her passes were not really progressive into the final third, and she carried more to progress. Her average carry speed was also high.

<a id="breakdown-of-results-by-players-lia-walti-arsenal"></a>

#### **Lia Walti - Arsenal**

<div align="right"><a href="#table-of-contents" style="color:black">↑ Back to table of contents</a></div>

Walti was clustered with advanced dynamic defensive midfielders, but only just. Most of her involvement positions, including receiving, dribbling, recovering, tackling, and winning fouls, were higher up the pitch, but her passing was mostly progressive into the final third. Although her average carry distance was longer, her average carry speed was rather low.

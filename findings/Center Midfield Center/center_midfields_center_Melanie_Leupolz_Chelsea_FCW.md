## **Findings**

Leupolz was safely clustered into k = 2 progressive distributors cluster and k = 3 deep lying playmakers cluster. Her passes were mostly long, forward, progressive, and into the final third, but not into opposition box. She involved in lower locations down the pitch, and did not receive much inside opposition box and engage much in counterpressing in opposition half.

In this project, there are a total of 56 center center midfields considered, with

- 21 center midfields in 12 teams in the English FA Women's Super League
- 19 center midfields in 12 teams in the German Frauen Bundesliga
- 26 center midfields in 16 teams in the Spanish Liga F
- 17 center midfields in 10 teams in the Italian Serie A Women

<a id="results-for-2-clusters-breakdown-of-results-by-features"></a>

#### **Breakdown of results by features**

- Cluster 0 midfielders made more progressive passes and passes into opposition box, carried more into the final third, defended at more advanced locations, in particular blocking and pressing.
- On the other hand, cluster 1 midfielders shot closer to goal at higher quality per shot, while standing out for percentage of header passes and shots. They received more inside opposition box, and their receiving locations varied more.

Therefore, based on these results,

- Cluster 0 center midfielders can be seen as progressive distributor.
- Cluster 1 center midfielders can be seen as aerial and box arrival threats.

<a id="results-for-3-clusters-breakdown-of-results-by-features"></a>

#### **Breakdown of results by features**

- Cluster 1 midfielders passed and carried more backward, while stood out for header features like shooting and clearing. Their receipt positions varied more, while receipts inside the box also stood out. Their shots were closer to goal and of higher quality.
- While cluster 0 midfielders passed and carried more forward and progressive into the final third, where their passes were longer on average. Most of their positionings were higher up the pitch, including for receiving, dribbling, and most defensive attributes.
- Cluster 2 midfielders, on the other hand, involved in lower positions both when attacking and defending, while dribbling and receiving more centrally.

Hence, the following conclusions can be made:

- Similar to k = 2 cluster 1, k = 3 cluster 1 comprises of aerial/box arrival threats midfielders, which can be seen as deep aerial specialists. Whether these players can be defined as box to box midfielders need to depend on heat map of locations.
- Cluster 0 midfielders can be seen as progressive distributors and carriers, which can be seen as advanced playmakers.
- Cluster 2 can be seen as deep lying playmakers.

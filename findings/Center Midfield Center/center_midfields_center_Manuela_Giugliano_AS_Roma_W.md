## **Findings**

Giugliano was clustered confidently with progressive distributors at k = 2 and advanced playmakers at k = 3. Her forward progressive passes and carries stood out for players of this cluster, and the passes can include those into the final third or even into opposition box. The lengths of these passes are also longer. She was also involved at more advanced locations up the pitch.

## **Findings**

Nusken was safely clustered into cluster with aerial/box arrival threats for both k = 2 and k = 3. She stood out for receipts in opposition box, header shots and clearances, quality of shots at closer distance, and backward pass share. Her receipt and defending locations were further up the pitch.

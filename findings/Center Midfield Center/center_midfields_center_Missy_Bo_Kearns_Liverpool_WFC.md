## **Findings**

Kearns was closely clustered into k = 2 progressive passers cluster, but then for k = 3 she was clustered with aerial/box arrival threats. She had moderate times of receiving in opposition box and header shots. Varying trends can be seen in her positioning, as she received, blocked, won fouls, and got dispossessed at higher locations, lower locations are places where she dribbled and made recoveries.

## **Findings**

Oberdorf was closely clustered with progressive distributors at k = 2, but was then clustered with deep lying playmakers slightly more confidently at k = 3. Most of her involvement happened lower down the pitch, while she stood out for long progressive forward passes, which can be either final third entries or even into opposition box. In contrast, she also had significant header shots.
